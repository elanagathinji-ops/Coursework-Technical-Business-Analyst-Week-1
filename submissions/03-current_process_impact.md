For a customer, their experience with the current process is frustrating, repetitive, and unclear.
Customers experience fragmented, high-friction contact journeys. They are often called repeatedly by different agents who have no record of previous calls or agreed payment promises. When transferred or calling back, they must re-explain their situation from scratch, lack digital self-service options to view balances privately, and face confusion over whether verbal promises have been processed

For a collections representative the process is overwhelming,convoluted and frustrating.
Representatives spend up to 50% of their workday hunting for information across 200-sheet spreadsheets, legacy database screens, and email trails before making a call. They constantly re-key the same data into multiple tools, rely on memory or sticky notes to track callbacks, and face customer frustration due to system-driven errors beyond their control.

For a team leader/ manager the current process is very stressful. They spend days manually reconciling activity counts between spreadsheets and database extracts that never match, chasing lost follow ups that slipped through shift hand offs, and defending unreliable numbers to finance and compliance directors.

Automation opportunities:
1. Automated account triage - accounts can be automatically categorized into straightforward or complex based on whether the customer has agreed on a method to pay the balance and is able to do so without hardship. Simple cases should be routed to the self-service portal and complex cases should queue for specialist representative handling
2. Automated self-service repayment - customers should be able to view their outstanding balance in a digital portal and be able to choose standard repayment options and set up an automatic recurring direct debit
3. Automated missed payment reminders - if a scheduled payment for an account has been missed, automated emails should be sent to the customers to prompt them to go into the portal within a certain time frame (1 day) and make their payment. If they fail to pay in then their account is flagged automatically in a central database and a representative is prompted to follow up with them.
4. Automated case update - when a payment is made/ missed, a customer disputes the balance , or submits a hardship request, this is automatically updated in a central database and flagged accordingly. 
5. Automated payment confirmations - when a customer pays in an installment on the portal, they automatically get sent an email receipt to confirm their payment
